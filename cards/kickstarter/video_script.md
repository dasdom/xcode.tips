# Xcode.tips

In 2021 I started to blog more about hidden features in Xcode.
These posts explained simple tricks and shortcuts that boosts the productivity of Xcode users.
But as their usage and usefulness was quite obvious, those posts have been short and boring.
So in the beginning of 2022 I was wondering if I could condense the essence of these tips and tricks into one drawn pages each, making them much more accessible.

The result was a drawn tip about navigating between editors in Xcode, a tip I stubbled across while I tried to be productive without a trackpad.
I posted the image on my normal blog and got some nice feedback about it.
This kind of presentation seemed to resonate with people.

And I was pleased with is as well, so I drew more.
I registered the domain Xcode.tips and started to draw one image every other day for more than two month.

The tips have been well received in the community and have been featured in iOS developer newsletters several times.

Right from the beginning I dreamed about putting these drawing onto learning cards.
A few month went by and here we are at this Kickstarter project.
If this project is successful, I will get a professional printing service to produce these card desks and sent them to you.

Back this project and get nice learning cards for your desk to become a better Xcode user!

Thank you for your time!
